Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: We use Git to coordinate version control and team collaboration to allow multiple team members to work on the same codebase simultaneously.


Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Finance- for example, data science could be used for market trend analyses to help financial organizations shape raw market data into a clear strategy. 


Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging keeps the project's commit history while rebasing rewrites the commit history to make it easier to read and debug. Rebasing should only be used on local branches.
